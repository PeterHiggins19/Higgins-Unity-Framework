# HUF Carrier-Filter — Dissemination Doctrine

**Status:** HUF-GOV governance doctrine · companion to the HUF Governance Charter, **Article IX (The Carrier Filter)** · 2026-06-07.

## Principle
**Deterministic = Hs. Dissemination = HUF.** The first stage of every case is the exact deterministic Hs response — for the data *and* for the channels. When a deterministic result must be **distributed**, that transition is a **governed breakpoint**: HUF filters the carrier on a **need-to-know** basis before the result travels. Hs measures; HUF carries; the filter is the breakpoint between them (both stages of the one tensor train).

**Posture: self-governance, not enforcement.** This is restraint chosen *for a reason* — because the breakpoint is understood — not a police force imposed from outside. HUF governs by internalized, reasoned self-control (accountable breakpoints, the right to interrupt, need-to-know carriers). That is why it scales and is adoptable: it protects without policing.

## The carrier filter — first cut: personal vs public
| Content | Carrier | Space |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal / private** — relationship-as-union, contact details, close-emotional register, not-yet-consented relationships | personal channel, need-to-know | **off-repo**, tracked (e.g. coworker `Personal-Contacts/`) |
| **Public** — deterministic science, public-professional citation, business, government | public channels, register-appropriate (collaborator / commercial / governmental) | **repos** (public) + the relevant public surface |

## Operating rules
1. **Repositories are public; no personal material in any repo.**
2. **Personal material lives off-repo and is tracked** (coworker `Personal-Contacts/`).
3. A *true* result is **not** automatically fit for every channel; fitness is decided at the filter.
4. **Strip personal from science / business / government carriers.** Public-professional citation (e.g. a researcher's published work) is public and may remain.
5. **Unconfirmed relationships are personal** until consented.
6. Applies to **all communications** — letters, repos, briefs, government packages, and AI-collective carriers alike.

## Why it fits real government
It mirrors **classification / need-to-know**: it respects privacy and public exposure simultaneously, by carrier, and prevents registers and plans from mixing in the wrong space. Easy to follow; easy to audit; fits every branch.

## Lineage
Generalizes the negative-case lesson (the emotional collaboration letter: *true, but the wrong carrier for a public/arms-length space*). That single observation is now a systemic rule — a system for dissemination, to sit beside the systems for analysis and provenance.

## Second filter dimension — the spectrum / level filter (meet the user's realm)
The carrier filter decides *which channel*; the **spectrum filter** decides *at what level and scope* content travels. A response should match the **compositional realm of the request actually made** — its dimension, depth, and intent — not the framework's full spectrum.

- **Answer the question asked, at the level asked.** The user who wants a cheeseburger is not asking for the cattle industry, the composition of ground beef, or a dairy-and-baked-goods lecture. Delivering that wastes tokens and money and yields a dissatisfied user who received an unwanted education they did not retain.
- **The failure is mutual.** Pitched above the user's realm, *neither* side is answering the same question; the loop repeats without converging — the repetitive-feedback trap, where more effort never reaches the desired result because the realm itself is out of reach.
- **The fix is a get-to-the-point filter.** Read the user's realm/intent first (the deterministic "what was actually asked"), narrow the field to it, answer there, and offer depth only on request.
- Same family as the carrier filter: a carrier-loss tool applied to **level** as well as **audience** — withhold the out-of-realm spectrum unless it is needed. Need-to-know, for depth.

**Benefit:** a good working space for everyone — the user gets the answer; the system spends its budget on the realm that matters; satisfaction rises because the question and the answer finally occupy the same space.

*The instrument reads. The expert decides. HUF carries only what the channel needs to know — and only at the level the realm requires.*
