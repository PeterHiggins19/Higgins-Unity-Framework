## Offer 1 — to space science: the work, and a way to get it back safely

Space science (remote-sensing arrays, constellations) is offered two things: **the work to be done** —
compositional reads of fleet, atmosphere, and instrument budgets — and **a way to get it back safely**. A downlink
in this scheme is **integrity-checked by construction** (the chained receipt catches any corruption or tamper
end-to-end), **compact** (D bytes per reading), and **self-describing** (it routes itself on arrival). Because
resolution climbs with accumulated frames to the coherence limit, the link returns *as much as the data and the
channel jointly support, and no false precision beyond it.* The science goes up as work to be done; the result
comes back small, self-checked, and honest. *(Connects to the public SpaceX constellation proposal + P-Ω.)*

## Offer 2 — a way to move large systems effectively

A large system is a budget of many parts. Read compositionally, it routes itself: each unit self-sorts to its
regime lane (the conveyor), each component holds a voice and can steer, and the lowest-coherence node is the
helmsman to attend to first. Moving the system effectively is then a matter of letting the parts report and route
themselves under a deterministic discriminant, with the operator holding the one fixed breaker. We expose the
method; we do not automate the human out of it.
